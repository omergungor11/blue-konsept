Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Vision X ideal for corporate headquarters and brand experience spaces?

Vision X delivers precise visuals, seamless integration, and reliable performance across executive suites, innovation centers, and conference room video walls, supported by advanced meeting room tech, direct view LED, and seamless video wall solutions.

Do you support content, not just hardware?

Yes. We provide curated artist content and guidance for custom content workflows

Is professional installation included globally?

Yes. Blue Square X deploys Vision X worldwide with expert installers.

What happens if a tile fails?

Extra spare components are included, and individual tiles can be replaced without disassembling the wall.

Which product option should I choose?
  • All-In-One: Preconfigured sizes, fastest deployment
  • Custom LED: Architectural, immersive, bespoke installs
  • Art Totem: Vertical, gallery-grade display formats
Can it be custom sized or curved?

Yes. Vision X supports fully custom dimensions, concave walls, and non-standard formats.

How thin and architectural can the install be?

Vision X installs flush to the wall at under 4 inches while maintaining proper airflow and ADA-friendly depth.

Is the screen HDR?

Yes. Vision X LED is HDR enabled, not just HDR supported. It’s built to deliver true dynamic range, not simply accept an HDR signal. Full HDR performance may require additional cabling and configuration to achieve the best visual results.

Is this a complete kit or just LED panels?

It’s a complete kit, including mounting, controls, spare parts, documentation, and support.

Is color consistent from any angle and distance?

Yes. Vision X maintains rich, accurate color from any angle and any distance in the room.

How does it perform for close-view environments?

Designed for near-field viewing, Vision X delivers smooth, pixel-free visuals at close distances with no visible seams.
